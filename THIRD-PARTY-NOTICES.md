## Natural Earth (public domain) via world-atlas (ISC)

The land and the country borders of the Map (§ 3.43) are Natural Earth 1:110m, a public-domain dataset (naturalearthdata.com), read from the TopoJSON of world-atlas 2 (ISC, Mike Bostock) and baked into `index.html` as rows of hex cells. Nothing is fetched at run time.
